Vizancia Support

Help with the Vizancia AI learning app for iPhone and iPad.

No account or login required

Contact us

Email info@vizancia.ca with a short description of the problem, your device model, and your iOS version. Please do not include private student information.

Quick fixes

  • Update Vizancia and iOS to the latest available versions.
  • Close and reopen the app if a game stops responding.
  • Check Settings inside Vizancia for sound, notifications, appearance, and learning preferences.
  • Use Reset Progress only when you intend to erase local learning progress.

Game and lab help

  • AI Startup: open the room controls, balance data, model capacity, and human review, then start the shift from the Strategy Desk. Pause or choose Relaxed speed whenever you need more time.
  • Prompt Lab: choose prompt chips, predict the reply quality, run the simulated prompt, and use the result feedback to refine it. All replies are pre-written and run offline.
  • If movement controls or a button stop responding, close the activity and enter it again. Your completed learning progress remains stored on the device.

Sending beta feedback

Feedback such as “Too easy,” “Just right,” or “Confusing wording” is saved only on the device. An adult may choose to export a report from Settings and decide where to send it. Vizancia never submits the report automatically. Please review it and remove any personal student information before sharing.

Include the app version

When reporting a problem, include the Vizancia version shown in Settings or at the bottom of the app's About screen, including the build number when available. The current release candidate is 4.0 (10).

Privacy and data

Vizancia has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking. Learning progress is stored on the device. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
